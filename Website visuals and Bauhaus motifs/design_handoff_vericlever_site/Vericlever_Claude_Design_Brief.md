# Design Brief: Vericlever Landing Page & Visual Identity

## What Vericlever is

Vericlever is a multi-tenant B2B SaaS compliance and staff management portal for Australian Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) providers. It positions itself as a light LMS that builds staff knowledge and capacity, not just a document sign-off tracker. Customers are childcare providers (Approved Providers, Centre Directors, educators) who need staff to read, understand and sign off on operational SOPs, with policies sitting behind the scenes carrying the regulatory and parent-facing obligations.

The working brand name is Vericlever (domain vericlever.com.au), currently pending trademark attorney review, so name treatments should be flexible rather than permanently baked into complex logo lockups.

## Scope of this task

This brief is for the landing page and supporting marketing visuals, plus early logo concepts. It is not a request to touch the working portal.

The portal itself (the logged-in product: dashboards, SOP pages, policy management, staff records, admin views) is functionally built and should not be redesigned or restructured. The only portal-adjacent elements in scope are navigation and menu styling, so that the public-facing site and the in-app experience feel like one product. Everything else in the portal is out of bounds for this piece of work.

In scope:
- Public landing page (hero, value proposition, how it works, pricing/contact)
- Supporting marketing visuals and imagery style
- Logo concepts and mark exploration for "Vericlever"
- Navigation/menu styling to bridge landing page and portal

Out of scope:
- Any portal screen, workflow, or data structure
- Naming decisions (name is still pending trademark clearance)

## Design direction: crisp Bauhaus

The reference is the attached Bauhaus Design 1926-style poster: concentric arcs and quarter-circle motifs built from repeated thin outlined rings, rendered in a single confident accent colour against an off-white background, paired with bold, simple sans-serif type. The appeal is precision and restraint, geometric pattern doing the decorative work rather than illustration or photography, with one strong colour carrying the whole composition rather than a multi-colour palette.

For Vericlever, the direction to explore:
- A single accent colour (not necessarily green, open to a colour that fits a compliance/education SaaS tone, but used the way the reference uses green: confidently, as the one graphic colour against a neutral background)
- Concentric arc / ring patterns as a recurring graphic motif, for example running along a page edge, behind a hero section, or as a subtle background texture, echoing the "single colour around the outside" framing effect in the reference image
- Clean geometric sans-serif typography, bold weight for headings, generous letter spacing where appropriate
- Flat, unfussy layout: strong grid, generous whitespace, no gradients, no drop shadows, no stock-photo people
- The overall feel should read as precise and trustworthy rather than playful, appropriate for a regulatory compliance product, while still feeling modern and light rather than corporate and dense

## Audience and tone

Buyers and users are Approved Providers, Centre Directors and educators in Australian ECEC. They are dealing with a genuinely dry regulatory domain (NQF, NQS, Reg 172, staff credentialing) and are often sceptical of software that overpromises. The tone of the landing page should be clear and credible rather than salesy: plain statements of what the product does, why the two-tier policy/SOP structure matters, and where the consultancy layer adds judgement software can't. Avoid hype language, exclamation points, or generic SaaS marketing clichés.

## Deliverables requested

1. Landing page design (desktop and mobile)
2. A small set of supporting visual assets in the Bauhaus pattern style, usable as backgrounds/section dividers
3. 2 to 3 logo concept directions for "Vericlever" using the same restrained geometric sensibility
4. Menu/navigation styling that ties the public site to the existing portal
